            Modules

            Monitoring and ATC read their setting at startup – restart Duopilo after switching. Input and voice loop apply immediately.

            • MonitoringCallouts and warnings as pilot monitoring
            • InputDuopilo operates switches – only after readback and confirmation
            • ATCAir traffic control by Duopilo (radio, handoffs, clearances)
            • Voice loopMicrophone always open, commands and radio straight to Duopilo

            First officer and small talk

            With the voice loop on, each flight gets its own first officer: a random name with a matching voice (never the voice of an ATC station). They introduce themselves at the briefing and also answer to their name. Optional small talk via a language model: only sentences that are neither a command, a query nor a radio call – callouts, warnings, checklists and radio always come first and interrupt it. The model never triggers anything. Applies immediately.
